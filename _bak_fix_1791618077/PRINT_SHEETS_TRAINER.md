# Trainer print sheet: what to print, cut and hand out

This sheet holds the print, cutting and distribution instructions that were taken off the participant pages (PREVIEW_CHANGE_NOTES.md, section 4). Participants do not see it.

## Day 1 Session 2: decision cards (`Oct12_session2_review_cards.qmd`)

- Print the four decision cards, one set per pair or group (adjust to room size).
- Explain the cards the first time they appear: each card shows one statement about GreenWaste and asks "Act, ask first or do not act?"
- For a shared room response use the existing A1 board (`Oct12_session2_board_A1.pdf`). The browser buttons on the cards record a choice in one browser only and do not collect room votes.

## Day 1 Session 3: age slips (`Oct12_session3_review_slips.qmd`)

- Print the 20 manager-age slips and cut along the dashed borders. Check the slips file for how many sets your room needs.

## Day 2 Session 3: profile cards and reveal slips (`Oct13_session3_review_cards.qmd`)

- Cut along the dashed borders.
- Hand out page 1 only (the seven business profile cards), one set per pair.
- Keep page 2 (the reveal slips) until the pairs have recorded their choices in Q1 on worksheet page 3. Then hand out one set per pair.
- Intended pairs: A with 2, B with 1, C with 3. Comparison business 4 is not used. Do not let pairs re-match after seeing the costs.

## Day 3 Session 1: scenario cards, board and results table (`Oct14_session1_review_cards.qmd`)

- Print one scenario card per group. For eight groups print Cards 1, 2 and 3 twice, so the allocation is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 1, 2, 3.
- Print the two-lane board (page 6) once per room, or use the existing A1 board (`Oct14_session1_board_A1.pdf`) for the full-room activity. The browser version is a reference only and is not a shared poll.
- Keep page 7 (the scenario results table) until every group has recorded its prediction in Q4 on worksheet page 4. Pages 1 to 6 do not contain the scenario results.
- The worksheet page 2 scenario settings stay closed during the predictions.

## Day 3 Session 2: no cards or slips

- Worksheet page 6 (prepared response) is for participants without Copilot. Tell them to open it after they finish Stage 1.
- Worksheet page 7 (numbers for reference) is opened after the two-business calculation in Q4.

## Timings given aloud (removed from the worksheets)

- AI exercise in every session: 15 minutes in total. Stage 1 (mark the response) 8 minutes, Stage 2 (run the improved prompt in Microsoft Copilot) 7 minutes.
